Engineering Operations Technician, Data Center Engineering Operations

Description
Technical support role ensuring uptime of mission‑critical data center infrastructure.

Summary
Amazon Web Services seeks an Engineering Operations Technician for its Vasteras data center (and other Swedish sites) to keep critical power, cooling and safety systems running at >99.999% availability. The role involves hands‑on maintenance, alarm monitoring, troubleshooting, and coordination with contractors, plus participation in events and knowledge‑transfer activities. Candidates need 2+ years of electrical/mechanical experience, a EU driving licence, and must work a 24/7 shift rotation. Certifications and data‑center experience are a plus. AWS offers an inclusive culture, work‑life balance, and growth opportunities.

Responsibilities
Maintain and monitor electrical, mechanical, HVAC, fire‑life safety and generator systems; respond to alarms and incidents; assist contractors and engineers with equipment deployment; perform site walk‑throughs and rack installations; coordinate vendor work; support multiple sites during events and planned work; share knowledge with new hires.
