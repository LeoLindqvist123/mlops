Engineering Manager

Summary
NOBA Bank Group seeks an Engineering Manager for its Stockholm software team, overseeing five engineers building secure loan onboarding services. The role blends hands‑on full‑stack development (C#/.NET, React) with leadership duties: coaching, cross‑team collaboration, technical guidance, and recruitment. Candidates need 7+ years software experience, 2+ years leadership, and strong Microsoft stack skills; cloud, TDD, CI/CD and Swedish are a plus. The company offers a culture of freedom, feedback, and growth within a supportive, values‑driven environment.

Responsibilities
Lead a team of 5 engineers delivering customer onboarding for Nordax, mentor and coach staff, drive cross‑team initiatives, ensure business goals and deadlines are met, guide technical decisions and process improvements, recruit and grow talent, and collaborate with product and technology leadership.
